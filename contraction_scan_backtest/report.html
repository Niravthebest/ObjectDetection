Same early-exit mechanics, but the stop/target distance is sized as a multiple of each stock's own ATR(20) at signal time, not a flat % of price — a quiet stock gets a tight dollar stop, a volatile one a wide one. Cell shows Sharpe, with CAGR and the resulting average stop % of price below (since the dollar distance varies per stock, this converts it back to an average % for comparison with the flat sweep above).

Methodology, universe construction, and every interpretation assumption made for this proprietary scan syntax are documented in the repo's contraction_scan_backtest/README.md. No slippage/commissions modeled; one position per symbol at a time; survivorship bias from using today's liquid-stock list as a static universe. Same-day stop/target ambiguity (both levels crossed in one bar) is resolved conservatively: stop assumed hit first unless the open already gapped through one level.
